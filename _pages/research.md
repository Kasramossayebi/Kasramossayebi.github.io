I study algebraic and homotopical structures that organize quantum information. A recurring theme is to replace a difficult state-by-state calculation with an invariant that is functorial, basis-independent, and computable.

## Current directions

### Algebraic K-theory for quantum resources

**Ongoing - IAIFI visiting student research, summer 2026**

I am applying Waldhausen's $S_\bullet$-construction to categories of quantum resources, with the goal of producing computable spectra and invariants for entanglement and quantum magic. This includes testing the construction on resource categories and comparing different delooping machines. A preprint is in preparation.

### $A_\infty$-algebras and entanglement

**Ongoing - since March 2026**

Using homotopy transfer, I extend the entanglement DGA to an $A_\infty$-algebra and study information carried by its higher operations. The project establishes local-unitary invariance of the transferred structure and extracts basis-independent invariants from higher Massey products. A preprint is in preparation.

### Hodge DGAs and deformation theory

**Independent, exploratory project**

I am developing a type-conscious framework for Hodge DGAs that separates cyclic or Frobenius pairings from positive Hermitian realizations. The longer-term aim is to understand when Hodge-theoretic positivity and semiregularity mechanisms can control deformation obstructions. These ideas are work in progress, not announced theorems.

## Published foundation

### Hodge theory for entanglement cohomology

With Christian Ferko, Eashan Iyer, and Gregor Sanfey, I developed a Hodge-theoretic framework for the entanglement complexes introduced by Mainiero. The paper proves structural compatibility results and gives a Hodge decomposition for entanglement cohomology.

[Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.111.032422) · [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12529) · [BibTeX](/publications/)

## Earlier work

### Generative effects and quantum entanglement

With Christian Ferko, I explored whether the categorical notion of a generative effect can detect entangled states. This led to questions involving Segre embeddings, von Neumann algebras, Haag duality, and descent in higher categories.

### Homotopical equivalence of relative categories

In an independent 2023 project, I studied conditions under which an adjoint equivalence of relative categories extends to a homotopical equivalence, building on work of Gurski, Johnson, and Osorno.
