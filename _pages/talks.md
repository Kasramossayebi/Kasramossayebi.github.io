## An Introduction to Entanglement Cohomology

**Physics Undergraduate Colloquium, University of Toronto - January 23, 2026**

An introduction to the GNS entanglement complex and entanglement cohomology, followed by a two-qubit example showing how the complex detects entanglement.

[Event page and abstract](https://www.physics.utoronto.ca/news-and-events/events/dept-events/an-introduction-to-entanglement-cohomology/)

## Generative Effects and Quantum Entanglement

**Mathematics Undergraduate Colloquium, University of Toronto - October 10, 2025**

A categorical perspective on generative effects, their relationship with entanglement cohomology, and possible extensions involving higher-category descent and operator-algebraic ideas.

[Seminar listing and abstract](https://seminars.math.utoronto.ca/pages/seminars?case=view_talk&talk_id=1760127000-1759714840-367)
