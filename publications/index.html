  1. Phys. Rev. A
    Hodge theory for entanglement cohomology
    Christian Ferko, Eashan Iyer, Kasra Mossayebi, and Gregor Sanfey
    Phys. Rev. A, Mar 2025
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib HTML PDF

    We develop a Hodge-theoretic structure for entanglement cohomology, including compatible adjoints, Laplacians, harmonic representatives, and a Hodge decomposition for the relevant complexes.
